Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

Revfa respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you visit our website, contact us, receive messages, or use our services.

Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Contact information, such as your name, email address, telephone number, business name, and job title.
  • Information submitted through forms, appointment bookings, emails, calls, and text messages.
  • Project information, files, content, credentials, preferences, and feedback you provide.
  • Website and device information, such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, referral source, and cookie data.
  • Payment and transaction information processed through payment providers.
  • Communication records, including consent records, call notes, and, where permitted, call recordings or transcripts.

How We Use Information

Revfa may use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide requested services.
  • Schedule appointments and communicate with prospective and existing clients.
  • Create and manage websites, advertising campaigns, lead-generation systems, and related projects.
  • Send account, project, support, appointment, and service-delivery notifications.
  • Send marketing follow-up only where consent has been provided.
  • Process payments, maintain records, secure our services, and comply with legal obligations.
  • Understand website usage and improve our services and marketing.

SMS Privacy

Your mobile information will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for promotional or marketing purposes.

All categories of information sharing described in this Privacy Policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Revfa may use telecommunications carriers and messaging service providers solely as necessary to deliver and administer messages requested by you. These providers are not permitted to use your SMS information for their own promotional or marketing purposes.

Message frequency varies, typically 1 to 10 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.

When Information May Be Disclosed

Except for SMS opt-in data and consent as described above, Revfa may disclose information:

  • To service providers that help operate our website or deliver contracted services.
  • When required by law, court order, or lawful government request.
  • To protect the rights, safety, property, or security of Revfa, its clients, or others.
  • In connection with a business restructuring, sale, or transfer, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

Revfa does not sell personal information.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies, analytics tools, and advertising technologies to operate the website, measure traffic, and understand campaign performance. You may control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling them may affect website functionality.

Data Retention and Security

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

International Processing

Information may be processed in Canada, the United States, or other countries where our service providers operate. Information processed outside your jurisdiction may be subject to the laws of that location.

Your Choices and Rights

You may request access to or correction of your personal information, withdraw consent where applicable, or request deletion subject to legal and contractual restrictions. To make a request, email nitish@revfa.com.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before consent was withdrawn. You can withdraw SMS consent at any time by replying STOP.

Children’s Privacy

Revfa’s website and services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

Revfa may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
