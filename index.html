Questions We Hear Often

What does Revfa build?

Revfa creates conversion-focused websites and the marketing systems that drive people to them. That can include positioning, design, development, paid advertising, lead generation campaigns, landing pages, follow-up, analytics, and optimization.

What is a marketing system?

It is a coordinated approach to attracting and converting customers. Revfa connects paid advertising, lead generation, landing pages, forms, follow-up, and tracking so every part supports the same growth objective.

How long does a project take?

Timing depends on the site's size, content readiness, campaign scope, integrations, and feedback cycles. After discovery, Revfa defines the milestones and launch plan before work begins.

Which platforms do you work with?

Revfa recommends the website platform, advertising channels, and marketing tools that best fit your goals, audience, team, and budget rather than forcing every business into the same stack.

Do you include SEO and analytics?

Projects can include technical and on-page SEO foundations, metadata, structured data, analytics, campaign tracking, and conversion measurement. Ongoing SEO or campaign management can be scoped separately.

What happens after launch?

Revfa can continue managing paid advertising, lead generation, testing, reporting, website optimization, and system improvements based on the ongoing scope your business needs.
